ABOUT ME

Building an engineering career through research.

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate with an M.Tech in Energy Engineering and Management from the National Institute of Technology Calicut.

My academic work has focused on thermal engineering, heat transfer, experimental investigation and computational modelling. During my M.Tech, I worked on improving thermal retention in flat-plate solar collectors through a modified absorber–glazing cavity configuration.

I am interested in pursuing advanced research where mechanical engineering, thermal sciences, computational methods and energy technologies intersect.

I am particularly interested in opportunities for Master's, Ph.D., research assistantship and engineering research roles.
